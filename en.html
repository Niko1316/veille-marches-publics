Frequently asked questions

Why pay, when the BOAMP and the EU journal are free?

They publish the notices, for free and for everyone. But the BOAMP does not know which notices concern you, it does not match the French notice with the identical EU one, it does not translate, and it does not put the deadline first. That is the work you pay for, so you no longer do it yourself.

How soon do I get my first alert?

Within 24 hours of payment. We set up your profile from your trade and your region, which you confirm by replying to our welcome email.

What if nothing suitable reaches me?

You write to us within 30 days. If you received fewer than ten tenders matching your trade, we refund you and you keep the alerts you received.

Can I cancel?

Yes, in one click, from the link in every email. No commitment, no questions, no call to make.

What do you do with my email address?

It is used only to send you your alerts. We never resell it or share it with anyone.

Can my criteria change?

Yes. Reply to any alert with your new criteria and they apply from the next day.
