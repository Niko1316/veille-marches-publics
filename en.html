Frequently asked questions

How soon do I get my first alerts?

Within 24 hours of payment. We configure your profile from the criteria you enter at checkout, and you can refine them by replying to our email.

How is this different from free alerts?

BOAMP and the EU journal do publish free alerts. They push everything, without deduplicating across sources and without regard to your trade. We sell the sorting, the translation and the deadline, not access to notices, which is public.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from the link in every email. No commitment, no questions.

Can my criteria change?
